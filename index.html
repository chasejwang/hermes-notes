About

This site is a personal writing practice. Each week, one essay — paired with an audio version rendered by MiniMax TTS in calm, considered male voice.

The aesthetic borrows from Indie Granola — warm cream surfaces, frosted glass, editorial serif for display, light weights throughout. No cold blues, no heavy shadows, no emoji.

Reading is first-class. Every essay has a print-friendly layout, a typographic scale tuned for long-form attention, and a quiet room to think.
